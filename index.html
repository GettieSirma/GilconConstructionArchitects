Questions

Good to Know Before We Talk.

Do you work in both Kenya and the USA?

Yes. We serve clients in both countries. Local codes, approvals and construction methods differ, so each project is planned around its jurisdiction.

What do you need to get started?

A short description of your goals, the site location, your timeline and a rough budget range. Site plans, title information or inspiration images help, but are not required for a first conversation.

Can you design and build, or only one of the two?

Either. You can engage us for design only, construction only, or an integrated service from concept to completion.

How is a project priced?

Pricing depends on scope, size, location and stage. After your consultation we confirm scope in writing before any work begins.

How is my personal information handled?

We collect only what you submit, use it to respond to your enquiry, never sell it, and store no cookies beyond what is strictly necessary unless you opt in. Read the or change your at any time.
